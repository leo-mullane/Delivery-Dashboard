What changed from V1

V1 is frozen as presented. Each change below is numbered to match the change log in the Plan of Action.

  1. 1. Overview leads with the three weekly measures: portfolio overrun, team utilisation against target by department, and at-risk projects.

  2. 2. Overview has a portfolio overrun tile with the over and under split, and a placeholder for the 12-month trend.

  3. 3. All views: "at risk" now means any overrun above 0%, for projects and contracts alike.

  4. 4. Header: "View as" is now a plain PM filter. Everyone in Level 10 sees everything.

  5. 5. Project: figures are labelled as they will come from Float (budget, logged, scheduled, forecast). Days to complete is gone.

  6. 6. People: utilisation against role targets, with a department breakdown.

  7. 7. Support: new Zendesk view.

  8. 8. Client: Zendesk panel added; account-manager notes removed.

  9. 9. Lessons and Project: project type shown on both, so matching is visible.

  10. 10. Assistant: default questions are the three weekly ones, plus a draft weekly SMT summary.

  11. 11. Pipeline: by client and by account manager, as two tabs.

  12. 12. Contracts: warning 60 days before a contract ends.
